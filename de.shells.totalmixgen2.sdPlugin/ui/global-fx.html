Refresh the list after changing the parameter or the bus: the names are read once, so a list fetched for another bus still shows its channels.

Range is 20 Hz to 20 kHz.

One step per press.

The key shows the chosen entry and lights while it is the active one. "Second press" is what a press does while the key is lit: nothing, go back to the entry that was active before, or switch to another entry (a two-way toggle). Reference levels are listed for the interface TotalMix reports (line inputs and outputs; mic and instrument inputs have none); with no device connected the list shows plain positions.

The knob fills across the parameter's range in the section's TotalMix colour and lights the section badge (EQ, D, LC, AL, FX, REQ) when that section is switched on. A custom key image or title set in Stream Deck replaces it.
